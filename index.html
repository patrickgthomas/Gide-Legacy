Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to buy anything to join my family?

No. Joining an existing family is free. Only the organizer purchases the family license.

Can Gide read our family memories?

No. Firebase coordinates membership and access, not family content. Stories and photos remain in your family's private vault.

I have an invitation link. What do I do?

Tap the private link your family organizer sent you. If the app is not installed yet, the link opens a page that walks you through joining.
